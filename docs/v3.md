# NurtureTrack v3: one household stream

Home is now one shared stream and one composer, so two parents do not need WhatsApp for the small things.

## What Home is

- Header: "Home" and one muted line with the greeting and the date.
- Composer: one wide input. Enter posts a plain message. The "+" opens a plain list: Log feed, Log sleep, Log nappy, Medicine (these deep link to `/care-log?log=<type>`, which opens the existing care log panel), Add a task (a small inline form that writes through the return to work tasks store). The Send control is the only solid plum control and appears only when there is text.
- One muted line under it: "Tonight: {name} on duty, handover {time}", from the shared sleep rota, left out when there is no rota.
- The stream: newest first, grouped by day, plain text on hairline rows. Messages from your partner show "Got it" until you acknowledge them, then "Seen by you". Your own posts show "Seen by {name}" once your partner acknowledges. Open tasks show "Done". Care entries are one line with no action. You can delete your own message with a "Delete" link that shows on hover or focus.
- A small coral dot beside "Home" in the sidebar (with visually hidden text "unread") while a partner message is unacknowledged.

## Where it lives

- `src/app/utils/stream.js`: pure `buildStream`, `groupByDay`, `unreadCount`. Unit tested.
- `src/app/stores/posts.js` and `src/api/posts.js`: the new `households/{hid}/posts` subcollection. Bound once in `useShared`, so Home and the sidebar dot share one listener.
- Rules: the `posts` block in `firestore.rules`. Fields in `docs/data-model.md`.
- No existing collection changed and nothing needs migrating.

## Not built

Push notifications. The stream relies on future push for alerts; nothing in the UI promises them.
